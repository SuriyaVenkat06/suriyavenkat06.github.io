06 / ACADEMIC GROUP PROJECTLCA REVIEW & DATA QUALITY

Checking the assumptions behind wool’s carbon footprint.

Critical review of Kasthall wool-carpet LCA and EPD methods, focusing on data representativeness, co-product allocation and transparent environmental reporting.

LCA / EPDData qualitySensitivity analysisEN 15804
Explore the project +

The challenge

Understand how methodological choices affect reported global warming potential for wool-based floor coverings.

Our approach

As part of a three-person Linköping University team, reviewed existing company LCA and EPD documentation, twelve literature sources and expert interviews. Compared allocation methods, geographic dataset representativeness, recycled-wool assumptions and climate metrics. This was a critical review, not a new full product LCA.

What we found

Identified sensitivity to economic versus mass-based allocation, limitations in upstream regional datasets and missing farm, scouring, transport and waste data. The report found upstream wool production to be the dominant climate-impact hotspot.

Recommendations and boundaries

Recommended consistent allocation choices, more representative primary regional data, transparent GWP100 reporting and sensitivity tests. Findings are a group academic analysis rather than a verified revision of Kasthall’s official EPDs. The underlying industry-linked report is not published here.
